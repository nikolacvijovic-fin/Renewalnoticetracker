# AI Risk Scoring Scope

This future layer is for read-only AI-assisted risk scoring over trusted workflow gaps.

Allowed future direction:

- risk buckets informed by reviewed truth
- decision-gap and owner-gap scoring
- confidence-aware risk surfacing for internal analysis or later gated product use

Not allowed:

- AI changing contract truth
- AI overriding review mode
- AI activating reminders
- AI bypassing acknowledgment, decision, or cycle gates

Activation requirement:

- scoring must be explainable from trusted workflow state
- scoring must carry explicit trust and confidence metadata
- scoring must never become hidden automation
