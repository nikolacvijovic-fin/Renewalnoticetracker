# Unit Economics Model

## Core Assumptions

- pricing is anchored to active tracked contracts and plan depth
- AI extraction, onboarding, import cleanup, and support are the main cost drivers
- good customers have meaningful contract volume and clear operational ownership

## Likely ACV By Segment

- Tiny SMB: $300-$900
- Operational SMB: $1.5k-$5k
- Midsize Ops-Led: $6k-$18k
- Enterprise-Lite CLM Seeker: $10k-$25k+

## Best Segment Economically

Midsize ops-led accounts with 50-500 employees and 150-800 active contracts.

## Worst Segment Economically

Tiny SMBs with weak urgency and enterprise teams that really want CLM.

## Major Margin Threats

- underpriced high-volume accounts
- support-heavy tiny accounts
- free or underpriced import cleanup
- mini-CLM drift

## Major CAC Threats

- broad paid acquisition before conversion is proven
- wrong-persona traffic
- enterprise pursuits with long cycles

## What Needs To Be True

- CAC must stay disciplined
- services must be tightly scoped and paid
- retention must come from real workflow embedding
- the team must reject CLM-like custom work

## Data To Collect

- ACV by segment
- support hours and onboarding time per account
- import cleanup burden
- extraction cost per account
- CAC by channel
- payback by segment
- NRR and churn by cohort
