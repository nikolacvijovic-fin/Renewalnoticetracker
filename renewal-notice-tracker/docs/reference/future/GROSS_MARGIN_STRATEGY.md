# Gross Margin Strategy

## Biggest Cost Leaks

- extraction-heavy low-value usage
- spreadsheet cleanup and onboarding handled as free support
- tiny accounts with high hand-holding
- custom CLM-like requests

## Biggest Support Burden Risks

- messy imports
- incomplete review and owner setup
- billing confusion from low-intent accounts
- pseudo-enterprise workflow demands

## Automate First

- import preflight and validation
- onboarding guidance and checklist logic
- extraction triage
- support diagnostics for reminders, billing, and imports

## Acceptable Manual Work Early

- paid onboarding
- paid import cleanup
- paid workflow setup
- quarterly reviews for high-value accounts

## Dangerous Manual Work Later

- unscoped import cleanup
- custom reporting
- repeated bespoke training
- one-off workflow design

## Margin-Destructive Customers

- tiny low-urgency teams
- CLM-seeking enterprise buyers
- messy-data customers who refuse paid migration help
- high-support low-volume accounts

## What To Cut

- custom CLM-like work
- bespoke analytics projects
- open-ended remediation work
- features with support burden but weak monetization value
