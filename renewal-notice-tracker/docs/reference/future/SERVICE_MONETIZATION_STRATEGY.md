# Service Monetization Strategy

## Core Rule

Services should accelerate SaaS adoption, contract coverage, and executive visibility. They should not turn the business into a low-margin contract operations agency.

## High-Margin Offers

### Onboarding Package

- target buyer: new Starter and Growth accounts with weak rollout discipline
- pain solved: delayed time-to-value after signup
- delivery: one-time
- why profitable: standardized kickoff and configuration
- retention role: gets the account to first operational habit fast

### Spreadsheet Cleanup / Import Package

- target buyer: teams migrating from messy spreadsheets
- pain solved: poor data quality blocking adoption
- delivery: one-time
- why profitable: repeatable cleanup and mapping workflow
- retention role: moves historical coverage into the product early

### Renewal Operations Setup Package

- target buyer: Growth accounts needing owner, escalation, and workflow discipline
- pain solved: unclear operating model after purchase
- delivery: one-time
- why profitable: high value, limited delivery scope
- retention role: embeds the product into recurring renewal operations

### Quarterly Review Package

- target buyer: Growth and Portfolio accounts with meaningful renewal volume
- pain solved: stale owners, stale decisions, stale data
- delivery: recurring
- why profitable: lightweight recurring review cadence
- retention role: keeps the product current and creates renewal conversations

### Reporting Package

- target buyer: finance, procurement, and ops leaders
- pain solved: manual executive reporting burden
- delivery: recurring
- why profitable: uses product data already captured
- retention role: makes the product visible to leadership

### Admin / Training Package

- target buyer: multi-editor and multi-team rollouts
- pain solved: weak internal adoption and admin confusion
- delivery: one-time
- why profitable: standardized enablement lowers support cost
- retention role: creates internal champions

## Offer Early

- Onboarding Package
- Spreadsheet Cleanup / Import Package
- Renewal Operations Setup Package

## Productize Later

- Quarterly Review Package
- Reporting Package
- Admin / Training Package

## Never Offer

- contract drafting or legal review services
- custom CLM workflow design
- embedded contract operations staffing
- unlimited spreadsheet cleanup with no scope cap
- bespoke analytics consulting outside renewal operations

## Ladder Into SaaS Expansion

- onboarding reduces failed rollouts and improves trial-to-paid
- import cleanup increases tracked contract coverage
- workflow setup supports Growth adoption
- quarterly review and reporting support expansion and renewal
- admin enablement supports rollout across teams and departments
