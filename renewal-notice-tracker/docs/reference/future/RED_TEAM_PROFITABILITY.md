# Red-Team Profitability Review

## Brutal Critique

The product has a real wedge, but it can still become an impressive contract-ops product that is not a strong business. The risk is not lack of intelligence; it is attachment to too many nice-looking capabilities and too much optimism about customer quality, retention, and services discipline.

## Hidden Profit Leaks

- meaningful free/trial load before buying intent is proven
- imports that quietly create delivery cost
- monetization that still leans on weaker feature gates

## Fake Monetization Logic

- overrating export and digest gating
- treating upgrades as success without checking support and margin

## Top 10 Profitability Mistakes Being Risked

- confusing activation with retention
- overestimating willingness to pay from small teams
- treating support-heavy imports as growth
- underpricing Growth coordination value
- letting services become custom consulting
- believing broad trials are harmless
- assuming all contract volume is equally valuable
- measuring top-line conversion without margin
- chasing enterprise buyers too early
- letting CLM adjacency creep into the roadmap

## What Must Change

- keep monetization centered on coverage and coordination
- scope messy imports and setup into paid work
- disqualify bad-fit customers faster
- track margin by segment, not just revenue
- make activation and retention milestones more explicit

## Leaner, Stronger Strategy

- sell to a narrower ops-led ICP
- win on renewal control, not broad contract management
- use services only to accelerate SaaS value
- cut features and customers that do not improve ACV, retention, or margin
