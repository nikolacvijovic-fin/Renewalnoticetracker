# Profitability Roadmap

This roadmap converts the current profitability strategy into an execution plan grouped by:

- pricing/packaging
- conversion
- retention
- analytics
- cost control
- expansion revenue
- GTM efficiency

Prioritization uses:

- `P1`: highest ROI
- `P2`: worthwhile, but secondary
- Difficulty: `low`, `medium`, `high`

## Service Strategy

### Onboarding Package
- Who buys it: Starter and Growth teams that need a fast rollout.
- Includes: kickoff, setup, owner model, notification setup, first dashboard walkthrough.
- Why profitable: low custom work and easy to templatize.
- Retention fit: accelerates first operational habit.

### Spreadsheet Cleanup / Import Package
- Who buys it: teams migrating from messy spreadsheets.
- Includes: cleanup, mapping, duplicate handling, first production import.
- Why profitable: high pain relief with repeatable delivery.
- Retention fit: gets historical data into the product early.

### Renewal Operations Setup Package
- Who buys it: Growth accounts needing real reminder and escalation workflows.
- Includes: offsets, escalation routing, owner model, templates, playbooks.
- Why profitable: small expert input creates large perceived value.
- Retention fit: embeds the product in weekly operations.

### Quarterly Review Package
- Who buys it: Growth and Portfolio accounts with meaningful contract volume.
- Includes: renewal risk review, owner gap audit, decision hygiene review, next-quarter actions.
- Why profitable: light recurring advisory with expansion value.
- Retention fit: reinforces a recurring product operating cycle.

### Reporting Package
- Who buys it: finance, procurement, and operations leaders.
- Includes: renewal exposure report, notice risk report, decision summary.
- Why profitable: built on existing product data.
- Retention fit: ties the product to executive visibility.

### Admin / Training Package
- Who buys it: teams rolling out across several editors or departments.
- Includes: admin training, role walkthroughs, SOP handoff, launch office hours.
- Why profitable: low delivery cost if standardized.
- Retention fit: improves adoption and reduces avoidable support burden.
