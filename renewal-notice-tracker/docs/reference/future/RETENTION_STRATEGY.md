# Retention Strategy

## Recurring Workflow

Customers keep paying when the product becomes the weekly place where upcoming renewals, notice deadlines, owners, and decisions are reviewed and acted on.

## What Makes The Product Sticky

- broader tracked contract coverage
- reviewed contract data that the team trusts
- clear owner accountability
- live due-soon and needs-review queues
- leadership visibility through reporting

## What Creates Habit

- reviewing due-soon renewals every week
- clearing contracts that still need review
- assigning owners to new and imported contracts
- updating renewal decisions before key dates

## Churn Risks

Early churn:
- no contract reviewed after upload
- no owner assignment after import
- no visible live obligation in the workflow

Later churn:
- workflow goes stale
- portfolio coverage stays narrow
- leadership never uses the reporting output
- the team falls back to spreadsheets

## Best LTV Levers

- owner-gap prompts
- decision hygiene prompts
- reporting loops
- team routing and accountability workflows
- expansion into more tracked contracts

## Core Metrics

Weekly:
- reviewed contract coverage
- owner coverage
- contracts needing review
- live obligations surfaced
- contract-count expansion

Monthly:
- logo retention
- expansion into higher contract bands
- renewal decision coverage
- reporting package attachment
- persistent workflow gaps
