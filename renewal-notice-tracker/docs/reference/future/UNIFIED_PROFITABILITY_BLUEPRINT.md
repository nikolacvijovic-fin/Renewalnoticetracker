# Unified Profitability Blueprint

## Target Customer
- Best target: ops-led SMB and midsize teams with real renewal exposure, meaningful contract volume, and moderate complexity.
- Best personas: finance ops, procurement ops, vendor management, legal ops-lite.
- Avoid: tiny low-urgency teams and enterprise-lite CLM seekers.

## Pricing Model
- Recommended model: workspace plus active tracked contract bands plus included editor seats.
- Starter: $99/month, up to 100 active tracked contracts, 5 editors.
- Growth: $349/month, up to 500 active tracked contracts, 15 editors.
- Portfolio: custom for 500+ active tracked contracts, governance, security, and implementation support.
- Push annual for Growth and Portfolio.

## Packaging Structure
- Entry: upload, extraction, review, owner assignment, renewal decisions, single-recipient reminders, dashboard visibility, import/export, templates.
- Growth: multi-recipient reminders, escalations, Slack/Teams, playbooks, broader team coordination, deeper admin depth.
- Top tier: custom contract bands, SSO, governance, approvals, security, guided rollout.

## Upgrade Triggers
- Active tracked contract cap pressure.
- Need for multi-recipient reminders or escalation chains.
- Need for Slack or Teams delivery.
- Cross-department rollout and more accountable editors.
- Executive reporting and quarterly review needs.

## Conversion Flow
- First session should get one contract uploaded, reviewed, owned, and visible with a live obligation.
- First-value milestone: one reviewed contract with an owner and a visible deadline.
- First paid-value milestone: broader coverage, more coordination, or richer routing/reporting need.
- Trial motion: 14-day trial for SMB and lower mid-market, with sales assist for high-fit active accounts.

## Retention Logic
- The recurring workflow is weekly renewal and notice review.
- Stickiness comes from more contracts under management, trusted reviewed data, assigned owners, and live obligations.
- The strongest loops are due-soon review, owner coverage, decision hygiene, and leadership reporting.

## Add-Ons And Services
- Early: onboarding/setup, spreadsheet cleanup/import, renewal operations setup.
- Productize later: quarterly review, reporting, admin/training.
- Never offer: drafting, legal review, bespoke CLM workflow design, embedded staffing, open-ended cleanup.

## Analytics System
- North star: active tracked contracts with reviewed dates and assigned owners in paying workspaces.
- Activation: one reviewed contract, one owner, one live obligation surfaced.
- Track the funnel from pricing page to paid, plus gate pressure, expansion, support cost, and gross margin by segment.

## Unit Economics Logic
- Best segment: ops-led SMB and midsize accounts with 50-500 employees and 50-1,000 active contracts.
- Bad segment: tiny SMBs and enterprise-lite CLM seekers.
- The model works only if onboarding is scoped, support stays disciplined, and contract coverage expands over time.

## Cost-Control Priorities
- Automate import preflight, onboarding guidance, extraction triage, and support diagnostics first.
- Keep setup, cleanup, and workflow configuration paid and tightly scoped.
- Cut features and customers that raise support cost without improving ACV, retention, or margin.

## Main Risks
- Confusing activation with retention.
- Treating import-heavy support as growth.
- Underpricing Growth coordination value.
- Letting services become consulting.
- Chasing enterprise CLM adjacency.
