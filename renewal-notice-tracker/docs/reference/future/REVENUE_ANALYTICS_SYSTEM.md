## Warning Thresholds
- Falling pricing page to signup over 2 review cycles
- Falling activation to checkout after stable activation
- Rising gate exposure without rising gate conversion
- Rising billing portal opens before cancellations
- Rising downgrades or cancellations by segment

## Likely Blind Spots
- Pricing traffic volume without quality
- Gate exposure without paid outcome
- Checkout starts without completion
- Logo plan mix without MRR mix
- Trial-to-paid without activation depth segmentation

## Vanity And Misleading Metrics
- Vanity: pricing page traffic volume alone
- Misleading: raw checkout starts without completion or segment quality

## Strongest Leading Indicators Of Monetization Health
- Activation rate
- Reviewed contract depth
- Owner coverage
- First reminder rate
- Contract-cap pressure
- Upgrade CTA click-through rate
