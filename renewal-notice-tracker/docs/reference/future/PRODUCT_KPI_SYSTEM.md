# Product KPI System

## North Star
- Active tracked contracts with reviewed dates, assigned owners, and live obligations surfaced.
- Why it matters: this measures actual renewal-control depth, not passive storage.
- Review cadence: weekly.
- Good: growing with account depth and paid growth.
- Bad: paid accounts exist but trusted operational coverage stays shallow.

## Activation
- Workspace activation rate.
- Definition: a workspace has uploaded, reviewed, owner-assigned, reminder-enabled, and surfaced one live obligation.
- Review cadence: weekly.
- Good: most qualified workspaces activate quickly.
- Bad: uploads happen, but review, ownership, and reminders do not follow.

## Top Product KPIs
- Reviewed contract coverage
- Owner assignment coverage
- First reminder creation rate
- Renewal decision coverage
- Live obligations surfaced per workspace
- Contract coverage expansion rate
- Upload to review conversion rate
- Review to owner conversion rate
- Time to trusted contract
- Saved-view and dashboard return rate

## Funnel KPIs
- signup to first upload
- upload to first review
- review to first owner
- owner to first reminder
- reminder to first renewal decision

## Adoption KPIs
- contract coverage expansion
- dashboard return rate
- active workspace rate
- import adoption rate

## Workflow KPIs
- reminder creation rate
- due-soon contracts with decisions
- owner coverage
- live obligations surfaced

## Value Realization KPIs
- time to trusted contract
- time to first live obligation
- reviewed contract coverage
- active tracked contracts under accountability

## Feature Adoption KPIs
- import adoption rate
- multi-recipient reminder adoption
- playbook adoption rate
- digest adoption rate

## Trust And Quality KPIs
- review correction rate
- low-confidence extraction rate
- contracts missing key dates

## Vanity Metrics To Ignore
- raw signup count
- total reminders sent
- total stored contracts

## Operating Rule
- Review funnel and trust KPIs weekly.
- Review feature adoption and deeper workflow KPIs monthly.
- Never use raw traffic, raw signups, or raw reminder volume as the main product success story.
