# GTM Strategy

## Best ICP To Acquire

Ops-led SMB and midsize companies with real renewal exposure and 50-1,000 active contracts under management.

## Recommended Motion

Use a hybrid motion:

- founder-led outbound for high-fit accounts
- partner referrals for warm, trusted intros
- self-serve trial for smaller serious buyers

## Best Messaging

- stop surprise auto-renewals without buying CLM
- replace renewal spreadsheets before the next notice window slips
- turn buried notice dates into owners, reminders, and decisions

## Best Demo Narrative

1. show the financial risk of missed notice windows
2. upload one contract
3. review one contract
4. assign one owner
5. surface one live upcoming obligation
6. show how coverage and team coordination expand value

## CAC Reduction Priorities

- narrow ICP and disqualify CLM buyers fast
- lean on founder-led outbound and partners first
- improve proof points around avoided risk and time-to-value

## Top 90-Day Actions

- rewrite homepage and pricing around renewal control
- build targeted outbound for finance ops and procurement ops
- package a short wedge-specific demo
- build partner/referral motion
- measure source quality through paid conversion and retention
