## Not Shipped First

Deferred or internal-only:
- PayPal as a public self-serve checkout or management provider
- Stripe as a self-serve or active customer billing provider outside legacy migration-only handling
- monthly digest delivery
- Slack and Teams delivery
- public API keys, scoped API tokens, customer webhooks, and OAuth app connections
- native calendar sync
- watched-folder intake
- advanced playbooks
- customer-facing health, readiness, or internal analytics dashboards
- advanced OCR metering
- granular enterprise RBAC
- governance-heavy customer dashboards
- playbooks and custom reminder rules
- retention health surfaces and broader analytics/runtime strategy dashboards
- customer-facing retention settings, legal hold, data residency, configurable deletion windows, and broad customer data export

Canonical deferred registry:
- [lib/product/deferred-capabilities.ts](lib/product/deferred-capabilities.ts)

- full CLM
- drafting
- redlining
- negotiation
- e-signature
- CRM orchestration
- ERP integrations
- data warehouse export
- SharePoint replacement
- customer-facing internal strategy dashboards
